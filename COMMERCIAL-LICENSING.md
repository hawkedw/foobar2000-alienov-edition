# Commercial licensing

Requests for commercial use outside the PolyForm Noncommercial license's permitted purposes will use the public **Commercial licensing** issue template in `hawkedw/foobar2000-alienov-edition` after that repository is separately authorized and created.

Future route: https://github.com/hawkedw/foobar2000-alienov-edition/issues/new?template=commercial_licensing.md

This route is planned, not an assertion that a remote repository exists. Do not post credentials, private code, personal contacts or confidential business documents. No personal email is designated for this channel. Describe the material/version, intended use, modifications, deployment scale and distribution scope in public-safe terms.

An issue, discussion or payment does not grant permission. A future agreement can cover only rights the project owner holds. It cannot grant third-party redistribution rights or imply that the complete private source or portable package is available for commercial licensing. Contributor rights require adequate separate grants before any future dual licensing.
